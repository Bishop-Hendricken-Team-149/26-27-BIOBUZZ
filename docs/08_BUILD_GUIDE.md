# 08 — HIVE-QUEEN v12.6 Build Guide (CAD `HIVE QUEEN v111426m`)

Generated by `cad/build_guide_v12.py` from the as-built BOM (`cad/out/HIVE_QUEEN_149_BOM_CHECKLIST.xlsx`), the CNC export and the pulley verification. Re-run it after any CAD change. Wiring is in `docs/06_WIRING_PLAN.md`; the robot frame is +X front, +Y left, +Z up (mm).

## 1. Order in this sequence

1. **CNC plates first (longest lead time):** upload the five files in `cad/CNC/` to **JLCCNC** as 5052-H32 aluminium, 6.0 mm (deck, 2 side plates) and 5.0 mm (2 shear plates); ask for the holes marked TAP in `cad/CNC/HOLE_TABLE.md` to be tapped M4. SendCutSend, OSH Cut and Protocase stock only .187"/.250", which does not fit this design.
2. **Wave 1 COTS** (structure, electronics, drive, belts) and **wave 2** (hardware, cables, couplers, flywheels): every `To Order` row of the BOM, with live links, pack sizes and packs to buy. McMaster rows (torsion spring, dowels) are priced on their site at order time.
3. **Filament** (section 2) and start printing while parts ship.

## 4. Hardware prep

* **Heat-set inserts** go only into printed parts: M4 x 8.1 into the 5.6 mm holes, M3 x 5.7 into the 4.0 mm holes. Soldering iron at ~245 C for PETG / ~260 C for PETG-CF, press square, let them cool under a flat tip. Never in aluminium: plate holes are clearance or tapped.
* **Fasteners** are counted from the CAD (BOM section 'Hardware'); each row says where its screws go. Through-bolts into aluminium get nylock nuts; screws into tapped plate holes get a drop of medium threadlocker.
* **REX shafts:** cut to the lengths in the BOM's shaft row (cut plan from the shop inventory); the intake hard-stop pins are REX offcuts (2x 32 mm, 2x 24 mm). Deburr the ends so bearings and couplers slide on.
* **Printed HTD pulleys:** an M3 nut goes in each captive slot before the grub screw; tighten the cup-point screw on a REX flat.

## 5. Assembly order

1. **Chassis:** Strafer rails (17-hole channels), rear and front crossbars, towers on quad-block pattern mounts.
2. **Drivetrain:** drive motors on their quad blocks inside the rails; thrust bearing into each axle miter's dished back; REX axles in two seated 1611 bearings; set the pinion phase by hand until the mesh turns without binding (CAD: zero-overlap window 9.35-9.6 deg).
3. **Lower electronics:** Control Hub and Expansion Hub on their printed mount plates, Pinpoint on its spacer (3x M4 into the channel, 2 overhang corners with nylocks, 2 thread-formed), odometry pods in their cradles, battery mount 1209 with 8x M4 on the rear crossbar.
4. **Intake:** uprights, bearing/stop plates (2x M4 each), floating swing arms with the 5 spoked TPU wheels, M7 in its cradle + sleeve (4x M4 x 12 into the gearbox), hard-stop pins, funnel wings, then the G407 lip blocker on its REX stub (left stub through the web bearing).
5. **Magazine:** J-chute (bond the split kit with epoxy, sand lips < 0.3 mm), shrouds, gecko arms and springs, M8 + sleeve, belts, indexer gate on its REX shaft, S3 on its bracket.
6. **Turret:** CNC deck on the towers; side plates with the printed foot brackets; shear plates on the printed standoff tubes (M4 x 35 left / x 40 right into the tapped side plates); M5/M6 saddles (4x M4 x 12 into the tapped deck); main shaft (REX) with the Rhino wheel on its Hyper Hub and on each side **plate A | 1313 hub | plate B** bolted with 4x M4 x 25 + nylocks (plates clocked 45 deg); counter-wheel arms and return springs; hood on its REX stubs; S6 in its cradle.
7. **Servos and couplers:** before tightening each 4001 coupler's clamp, power the servo to its calibrated rest position (`HiveQueenHardwareTest`), hold the mechanism at its rest stop (gate closed, blocker stowed, hood at 62 deg), then clamp.
8. **Sensors:** throat Color V3 (slot 1), ballCount1 (slot 2), ballCount2 (slot 3) behind the left shroud windows; lip Color V3 at the mouth; HuskyLens on its L-bracket (2x M3 x 12 + nylocks through the DS plate).
9. **Wiring:** per `docs/06_WIRING_PLAN.md` (cable SKUs listed per run); switch in its guarded cage; label both ends of every cable.
10. **Signs:** slide the red or blue plate into the rails for each match.

## 6. Bench checks before the first match

- [ ] Run `HiveQueenHardwareTest`: each servo sweeps only inside its match limits; motor directions and encoders read (flywheel encoder fault shows on telemetry).
- [ ] Hood: protractor at the exit; calibrate `HOOD_CAL_LOW_POS` so the servo position for 62 deg and 72 deg matches (1 deg servo = 1 deg hood through the coupler).
- [ ] Springs: counter-wheel squeeze on a POLLEN and gecko return; swap sizes from the extension-spring kit until both hold.
- [ ] Ball sensors: with 1, 2, 3 and 4 POLLEN queued, telemetry count must match; NECTAR at the lip must be classified by size.
- [ ] Lone-ball feed: fire a single POLLEN and a single NECTAR 5x each from a closed gate. With 1-2 balls aboard they rest on gecko 1 (no wheel touches them for ~138 mm), so gecko 1 must throw them up to gecko 2 (MAGAZINE_INDEX_POWER 0.6 = ~690 RPM vs ~425 needed); raise it if a ball stalls.
- [ ] **R203:** with the robot powered off, every ball must be removable by hand (gate and blocker back-drive; magazine belts turn by hand).
- [ ] Sizing: stowed in an 18 in (457 mm) box; expanded within the limits in docs/05.
- [ ] Weigh the robot (CAD estimate 12.39 kg); check the CG by tipping on a ramp.
- [ ] Shot table: verify 0.75 / 1.00 / 1.25 m shots with POLLEN and NECTAR; 1.25 m is at the 1050 RPM cap (no margin).
